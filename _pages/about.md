## Who I Am

I'm **Rajnish Maurya**, an M.Tech Data Science & Artificial Intelligence student at **IIT Madras**, building at the intersection of LLM research, multimodal AI, and production-ready MLOps.

My work spans **mathematical LLM evaluation and LoRA fine-tuning**, **vision-language foundation models for trajectory prediction** (Bosch Global Software Technologies internship), and **end-to-end MLOps pipelines** with FastAPI, Docker, MLflow, DVC, Prometheus, and Grafana.

I care about connecting rigorous research with reliable engineering — strong baselines, systematic evaluation, efficient fine-tuning, and clean deployment workflows.
