During my Applied Research Internship at Bosch Global Software Technologies, I worked on trajectory prediction using vision-language foundation models.

**Highlights**

- Developed trajectory prediction models using fine-tuned vision-language and multimodal models.
- Conducted literature reviews, prototype design, and model evaluation.
- Achieved RMSE approximately 3 for trajectory prediction tasks with 60 predicted points over x, y, and z coordinates.
