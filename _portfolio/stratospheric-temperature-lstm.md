This B.Tech project used deep learning for climate time-series forecasting.

**Supervisor:** Dr. Uma Das, Department of Computer Science and Engineering, IIIT Kalyani.

**Highlights**

- Implemented an LSTM-based RNN to predict stratospheric temperatures at various pressure altitudes.
- Used ERA5 reanalysis data containing 51 years of monthly mean zonal temperatures from 1979 to 2019.
- Trained the model on 1979–2017 data and generated predictions for 2018–2019.
