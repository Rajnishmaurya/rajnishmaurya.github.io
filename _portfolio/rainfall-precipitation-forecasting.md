This B.Tech project explored climate forecasting at specific latitude-longitude coordinates.

**Supervisor:** Dr. Uma Das, Department of Computer Science and Engineering, IIIT Kalyani.

**Highlights**

- Developed a climate change forecasting model to predict precipitation rates at 0.25° resolution.
- Used historical data from 1998 to 2019.
- Applied ARIMA, SARIMAX, and LSTM models to estimate rainfall for the following year.
